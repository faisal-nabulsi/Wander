## ✨ Features

**Move anywhere**
- 📍 **One-tap Teleport** — search an address, paste coordinates or a Plus Code, drop a pin, or spin a 3D globe and tap to land.
- 🕹️ **Live Joystick** — a 360° on-screen stick that moves your location in real time, with walk, run and drive presets and a speed slider.
- 🛣️ **Multi-stop Routes** — chain any number of stops into one trip (on iPhone, free-plan multi-stop routes count toward the 3 a month; Wander Desktop makes them Pro) with snap-to-roads, **speed & ETA**, smooth motion, and auto-loop _(Pro)_. **Drag to reorder** waypoints and **reverse** the whole route in one tap.
- 🚗 **Transport modes** — pick how a route is traveled: **Driving**, **Walking**, **Cycling** _(Pro)_, **Public transport** _(Pro)_, or **Plane** (a great-circle flight path; simulated climb, cruise and descent altitude on Android).
- ✈️ **Flight planner** — pick two searchable airports, or add a time range to pick from generated example flights, then fly the great-circle route. Uses a bundled airport dataset — no API.
- 🎮 **Games mode** _(free on iPhone; Pro in Wander Desktop)_ — after each jump, a **timer** shows a suggested wait based on how far you moved, with an optional lock until it ends, plus presets for popular location-based games (full list in the app). It can't stop a game from warning or banning your account.

**See before you go**
- 👀 **Street View preview** _(Pro)_ and **satellite / hybrid** map views to land in exactly the right spot.
- 🗺️ **Offline maps** — download a map region over Wi-Fi, then pan, zoom and teleport with no connection (map tiles © CARTO, map data © OpenStreetMap contributors).
- 🕒 **Local time & weather** at the location you set, with a heads-up when your phone's time zone doesn't match it.

**Find & organize**
- 🔎 **Smart search** with autocomplete, plus **AI natural-language teleport** _(Pro)_ — "take me to Times Square."
- ⭐ **Favorites** in folders with tags & notes, **teleport history** with undo, and **save-loops** _(Pro)_.

**Plan & automate**
- ⏺️ **Record & replay** _(Pro)_ a real route, or queue a **timed itinerary** _(Pro)_ ("stay 1hr, then route to there").
- 📅 **Scheduling** — turn a saved location on and off automatically at times you choose. Runs even when the app is closed on Android; while backgrounded on iOS.
- 📡 **Geofence triggers** — switch back to your real location when you reach or leave a place you pick. _(iOS &amp; Android)_
- 🧠 **AI day planner** _(Pro)_ — describe a day (or tap surprise-me) and it suggests a day of stops. Plus a read-only board of current in-game events.

**Movement options**
- 🌊 **Distance-jump smoothing** — big teleports glide over a few seconds instead of snapping across the map. _(iOS &amp; Android)_
- 🌧️ **Weather-aware pacing** — an optional setting slows routes down in rain and snow. _(iOS &amp; Android)_
- 🧊 **Privacy toggles** — **Hold-still** (natural drift off on iPhone, **Hold perfectly still** on Android) and **Approximate (~3–5 km)** location modes. _(iOS &amp; Android)_
- 🛰️ **GPS drift** — small random movement around a held spot, on by default (on iPhone, turn off **Simulated jitter (natural drift)** in Settings to stop it; on Android, turn on **Hold perfectly still**).
- 🌐 **IP note** — a short explainer that apps can also estimate where you are from your IP address. _(iOS; desktop has the same IP card)_

**Your data**
- 📂 **GPX, KML, GeoJSON &amp; CSV import**, **GPX export**, full **backup / restore**, and **sync** of saved places and routes across your devices _(Pro)_.
- 🌐 **10 languages** — English plus partial translations into Spanish, French, German, Portuguese (Brazil), Italian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese (Simplified) and Russian.

**Setup & updates**
- ↩️ **Back to real location** — one tap returns your phone to its real location, plus **Dark Mode**.
- 📱 **Quick Settings tile & home-screen widget** for one-tap teleport _(Android)_.
- 📶 **Cellular support** — start the connection with a quick Airplane Mode toggle, then it keeps working on cellular without Wi-Fi.
- 🔓 **No jailbreak** — runs on a normal iPhone with Developer Mode turned on.
- 🔄 **Over-the-air self-updates** — after setup it updates and re-signs itself from the phone; keep your Apple ID signed in inside Wander and open it with the tunnel connected every couple of days. It re-signs itself at launch once 2 days or fewer are left on the free 7-day signature, and you can also run self-refresh by hand in Settings.

Start on the **free plan**: 1 teleport every day, plus 15 minutes of joystick and 3 routes every month. **Wander Pro** ($3.99 a month, $36 a year, or $80 once) makes them unlimited and adds Cycling and Public transport routes, auto-loop and saved routes, route recording, timed itineraries, AI teleport and the AI day planner, Street View, and sync.

## ❓ FAQ

**Is Wander free?**
There is a free plan that refills: 1 teleport every day, plus 15 minutes of joystick and 3 routes every month. **Wander Pro** makes those unlimited and adds Cycling and Public transport routes, auto-loop and saved routes, route recording, timed itineraries, AI teleport and the AI day planner, Street View, and sync, for **$3.99 a month, $36 a year, or $80 once**. One Pro account covers iPhone, Android, Mac and Windows on up to 3 devices.

**Does it need a jailbreak?**
No. Wander runs on a **stock iPhone** with Developer Mode turned on, with no jailbreak and no paid Apple developer account.

**Which platforms does it support?**
iPhone (iOS), Android, macOS and Windows — one account across all of them.

**Is Wander an alternative to iMyFone AnyTo, Tenorshare iAnyGo, or AnyGo?**
Yes. It does the same core things — teleport, live joystick walking, and multi-stop routes with no jailbreak — with a free plan that refills and lower prices than AnyTo's VIP plan on every billing period (as listed on 15 November 2026). See the [comparison table](#️-wander-vs-imyfone-anyto) above.

**Can I use it with location-based games?**
Yes, with limits. Wander changes the location your phone reports and doesn't modify any other app, so most apps see the new location. Some games check whether a location is simulated and may not accept the location Wander sets. Games mode adds a timer that suggests how long to wait after each jump; it's only a suggestion. Many games don't allow changing your location, and using Wander with them can lead to warnings, suspensions or a permanent ban, which Wander can't prevent. **Check the rules of each game you play.**

**How do I install it without the App Store?**
About 10 minutes the first time on iPhone (a one-time setup with a Mac or Windows PC), guided by the free **Wander Installer**. Android takes a couple of minutes. Start at **[wanderspoofer.com](https://wanderspoofer.com/#download)**.
